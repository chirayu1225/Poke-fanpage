Weedle

Weedle is a dual-type Bug/Poison Pokémon. It evolves from Caterpie starting at level 7 and is the final form of Weedle. Weedle is known for its sharp stingers and its ability to blend in with its surroundings. It has a gentle nature and is often seen crawling on the ground, searching for leaves to eat.
