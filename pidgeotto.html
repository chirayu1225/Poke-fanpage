Pidgeotto

Pidgeotto is a dual-type Normal/Flying Pokémon. It evolves from Pidgey starting at level 18 and is the final form of Pidgey. Pidgeotto is known for its ability to fly swiftly through the air and its keen eyesight. It has a gentle nature and is often seen soaring through the skies, using its wings to navigate with ease.
