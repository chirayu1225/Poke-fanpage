raichu

Raichu is an Electric-type Pokémon. It is a larger, more powerful version of Pikachu, with a more robust build and enhanced electrical abilities. Raichu is known for its ability to generate and control electricity with great precision.

Evolution

Raichu evolves from Pikachu when it levels up with a Thunder Stone.

Abilities

  • Static: May cause paralysis if the opponent makes contact with Raichu.
  • Lightning Rod (Hidden Ability): Draws in all Electric-type moves to boost Raichu's Special Attack.
